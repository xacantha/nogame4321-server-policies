# Nogame4321 — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 13, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the Nogame4321 Discord server and its custom bot. “We,” “us,” and “our” refer to the server owner and administration team.

## 1. Information We Process

Depending on the features enabled, the server and its bot may process:

- **Account information:** Discord user ID, username, and display name.
- **Server information:** Server ID, channel IDs, roles, and membership information.
- **Interactions:** Messages, commands, and content you submit.
- **Moderation records:** Warnings, reports, and actions taken by staff.
- **Activity information:** Information needed for enabled features, such as leveling and role assignments.

## 2. Why We Use This Information

Information is used to operate the server and its bot, including to:

- Respond to commands and provide server features.
- Assign roles and manage participation.
- Prevent spam, enforce rules, and address reports.
- Review moderation actions and support requests.
- Organize hide and seek games and server activities.

## 3. Who Can Access Information

Content posted in server channels is visible to people with access to those channels. Authorized staff may access moderation records and information needed to manage the server.

Avoid sharing sensitive personal information in messages or bot commands.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The server operates on **Discord**, and its custom bot is powered by **MEE6**. These providers process information under their own privacy policies:

- [Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy)
- [MEE6 Privacy Policy](https://mee6.xyz/privacy.html)

This policy describes the server team’s practices and does not replace those providers’ policies.

## 5. Information Sharing

Relevant information may be shared with authorized moderators to manage the server and address reports.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to address threats, abuse, or security incidents.

## 6. How Long Information Is Kept

Messages may remain in Discord until deleted. Moderation records may be retained to enforce rules and review repeat violations.

Discord and MEE6 manage retention within their own systems. Leaving the server does not automatically delete previous messages or moderation records.

## 7. Access and Deletion Requests

You can manage your Discord privacy settings, delete your own messages where permitted, stop using the bot, or leave the server.

To request access to or deletion of information held by the server team, contact **@speedysingh or @xacantha**. We may need to verify that you own the account concerned.

Some records may be retained for moderation, security, or legal reasons. Requests for information controlled by Discord or MEE6 may need to be submitted directly to them.

## 8. Security

We use available access controls to restrict staff-only information to authorized team members. No online service or storage method is completely secure.

Never share passwords, bot tokens, or other login credentials in the server or through the bot.

## 9. Age Requirements

Users must meet Discord’s minimum age requirement for their country.

Report concerns about underage users to the moderation team without posting their personal information publicly.

## 10. Policy Updates

We may update this policy and will announce significant changes in the server. The date above will reflect the latest revision.

## 11. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact **@speedysingh or @xacantha** in Discord.
